Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bottor Technologies Inc ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with WiggleWoo's Word Quest (the "App"), a phonics and early-reading game designed for children ages 4–8.

The short version: WiggleWoo's Word Quest does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All progress and settings are saved only on your own device. We don't use ads, third-party analytics, or trackers of any kind.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information from users of the App, including children. The App does not require account creation, sign-in, or any entry of names, email addresses, or other identifying information to play.

How the App Stores Data

WiggleWoo's Word Quest runs entirely on your device. Game progress, settings, and unlocked content are saved using your device's local storage only. This information:

  • Never leaves your device or gets sent to us or anyone else
  • Is not linked to any identity, account, or profile
  • Can be cleared at any time by removing the App or clearing its local data

Advertising and Analytics

The App contains no advertising, no third-party advertising networks, and no third-party analytics or tracking software development kits (SDKs).

In-App Purchases

The App is free to download and does not currently offer any in-app purchases. If that changes in a future update, this policy will be updated accordingly, and purchases will always go through Apple's App Store purchasing system.

Children's Privacy

WiggleWoo's Word Quest is designed for young children and built with children's privacy as a core requirement, not an afterthought. Because the App collects no personal information from any user, there is no personal information collected from children to begin with. Certain screens intended for parents and caregivers (such as learning progress reports) are protected by a simple parental gate so that young children cannot access them unintentionally.

Third-Party Services

The App does not share information with third parties because it does not collect any information to share.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the revised policy at this same address.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@wigglewoo.app.
